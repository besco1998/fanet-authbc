# S01 — capacity at the loss the paper's margin rule requires (97 % delivery)

*Registered 2026-10-27, before any run. Review of 2026-10-27, item S-01; Mohamed chose new ns-3
runs over interpolation or the model alone.*

## Why

The headline capacities (35 → 124 nodes) are crossings of 95 % delivery: frame loss p = 0.05,
exactly the zero-margin point of the target V ≥ 0.95. The paper's own rule sets the budget
ε ≥ p + 0.02, so at ε = 0.05 a deployment must run at p ≤ 0.03. The review asks for the
capacity there.

## What is run

* **Cells:** C (per-record signing, frame-linked, 146 B at 50 frames/s) and D (AUTHBC, 173 B at
  12.5 frames/s).
* **Channel and source:** the standard window and the designated source (one period), exactly
  as for the headline.
* **Runs:** 30 seeds per node count, 20 s.
* **Grid:** `experiments/nmax-direct/plan_level97.txt`, C at N = 25–31 and D at N = 86–104, step 3.
* **Command:** `ns3/run_nmax_direct.py --out-stem ns3_level97 --no-summary`.
* **Crossing:** the 30-seed mean delivered fraction is interpolated at 0.97
  (`analysis/capacity_at_97.py --score`).

## Predictions

### The contention model

`analysis/capacity_at_97.py --predict` → `results/raw/capacity_97_predictions.csv`, the same
event simulation, window and seeds as the F5 predictions at 2 % and 10 %:

| cell | model crossing at 0.97 | band ±3 % (F5's tolerance) |
|---|---|---|
| C, per-record signing | 27.53 | 26.70–28.35 |
| D, AUTHBC | 97.89 | 94.95–100.82 |

**Scored as:** each ns-3 crossing inside its band. The capacity ratio D/C is reported; predicted
3.56.

### Mine `[agent]`

* S01-1: the ns-3 ratio at 97 % lies within 3.3–3.8. It is 3.53 at 95 %, and the 2 % and 10 %
  F5 levels gave similar ratios.
* S01-2: at the existing points that repeat earlier runs (C at 25 and 26, same seeds, same binary),
  the delivered fraction is identical to `ns3_rule_levels_runs.csv`.

## What the paper will say

At the 3 % loss that recommendation (5) requires, the capacities are the ns-3 crossings, with
the ratio, beside the 95 % headline, which stays. The values are generated by
`analysis/paper_numbers.py` from `results/raw/capacity_97.csv`.
