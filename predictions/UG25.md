# U-G25 — (G25), the periodic-source loss expression, on new ns-3 cells (802.11b and 802.11a)

*Registered 2026-11-20, before any U-G25 run; the time is the commit's. Work package B of
`docs/prompts/NEXT_PHASE_PROMPT.md` (Mohamed, 2026-11-19: "U1: go ahead").*

## Why

Both failures of the 2026-11-18 validation report have one cause: the loss expression (G5)
assumes Poisson frame starts.
* **Periodic sources** are less clustered, and (G5) was pessimistic: 4.97–6.34 % at 802.11b for
  15–37 stations (`predictions/UD.md`).
* **Frames sent back to back** are more clustered, and (G5) was optimistic (`predictions/UC4.md`).

(G25) makes the sending pattern an input.

## The model under test (`src/authbc/models/periodic_capacity.py`)

In (G4), the number of co-waiting frames K is scaled by the ratio of the mean backlogs of the
N·D/D/1 queue and the M/D/1 queue, from the Brownian-bridge tail
P(Q > x) ≈ exp(−2x(x/n + 1 − ρ)):

    c = (1 − ρ)·sqrt(n/2)
    R(n, ρ) = 2(1 − ρ)·sqrt(π n / 8)·exp(c²)·erfc(c)
    K_per = R(N − 1, a + γ)·K          (K, a, γ as in G4; G5 otherwise unchanged)

* **The capacity** is the root of the loss in N, with p = ε inside a (bisection on a real N).
* **Every test vector** of the prompt's §3 is reproduced, and the module agrees with Mohamed's
  `analysis/periodic_model.py` (`tests/unit/models/test_periodic_capacity.py`).
* **Its stated limit,** given before any test: R holds for about 8 or more other stations. Every
  crossing below has 10 or more.

* The driver's cell list, `ns3/run_unified.py`, has none of them.

**The loss targets:** ε = 0.05 and **0.02**. At the lower target the crossings fall at lower N and
lower load, where R departs further from 1.

## What is run

* **The scenario:** the audit twin `ns3/authbc-links-audit.cc`, rebuilt on 2026-11-20. Its
  default path is gated bit-identical to `authbc-links.cc`, and to the frozen results it checks,
  before any run (`ns3/verify_audit_twin.py`).
* **The source:** the designated one, periodic with a jitter of one period (`--txJitterMs` at one
  period), so each frame is drawn anew in its period. 20 simulated seconds.
* **The pilot:** 5 seeds (1–5) on seven node counts at ±5 % steps around each (G25) root. Each
  target has its own grid; a node count shared by the two grids is run once.
* **The measurement:** 30 seeds (1–30) on seven node counts by U-C2's rule, around each pilot
  crossing:
  * the centre is the pilot crossing rounded;
  * the step is max(1, round(2 % of the centre)).
* **The crossing:** the node count at which the 30-seed mean delivered fraction crosses 1 − ε, by
  straight-line interpolation. Its bootstrap 95 % interval is taken over seeds (`ns3/run_unified.py
  --levels 0.95 0.98`), as every earlier U-* crossing was.
* **The co-waiting count** (work package B, item 4):
  * the loads: four node counts per cell, round(0.5, 0.75, 1.0 and 1.25 × the ε = 0.05 (G25)
    root); 10 seeds each, with `--traceMac`;
  * K measured as in U-C1 (`analysis/unified_c1.py`): for each frame that arrives to a busy
    medium, the number of other stations holding a frame when that busy period ends, averaged
    per run;
  * reported against R·K of (G25) and K of (G4), each at its own model's fixed point. The ratio
    measured over predicted is given with its spread over seeds.
* **Scoring:** `analysis/ug25.py --score` → `results/raw/ug25_scored.csv`.
