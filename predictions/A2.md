# A2 — the design over record rate × deadline, and age of information under loss

*Registered 2026-10-19, before any ns-3 run of the cells below and before any run of the
age-of-information harness at a registered setting. Plan: `docs/AUTHBC Revision Plan.md`, A2.
Execution notes: `docs/revision/EXECUTION_PLAN.md`. Never edited after the first run; the
outcome is appended below a line at the end.*

## Why

At both of the paper's operating points Λ·D_max = 5, so b ≤ 4 follows from the point and not
from the design. A reviewer can say the headline holds only there. This lays the design over
Λ ∈ {10, 20, 25, 40, 50, 75, 100} records/s × D_max ∈ {40, 60, 80, 100, 150, 200, 250, 500} ms.

## What is computed and what is run

* **The map (computed, not a prediction).** `analysis/design_map.py --predict` →
  `results/raw/design_map.csv`. Per cell: b* = the largest b with b/Λ + T_ch + t_v ≤ D_max,
  T_ch = 3.4907 ms (worst channel delay simulated at the design's crossing) and t_v =
  0.2595 ms (one Ed25519 verification on pi-B), both read from
  `results/raw/freshness_budget.csv`; the lean frame the codec EMITS with b* records at that
  record spacing (generator walk at `round(1000/Λ)` ms: 13 ms at 75 /s); bytes per record; and
  N_max of baseline and design by the access-rule model (`authbc.sim.dcf_unsaturated`,
  nothing fitted, 30 seeds per point, interpolated crossing of V = 0.95).
* **ns-3 validation (run after this commit).** Ten cells chosen by the plan — the four corners
  (10, 40), (10, 500), (100, 40), (100, 500); the inner diagonal (20, 60), (25, 80), (40, 100),
  (50, 150), (75, 200); the control (50, 100) — and **one cell beyond the plan**, (100, 100),
  the plan's own example of a large gain, so that "ratio > 5×" is simulated and not only
  modelled. Each cell's baseline (one record per frame at Λ) and, where b* ≥ 2, its design.
  `ns3/run_nmax_direct.py --plan experiments/design-map/plan.txt --out-stem ns3_design_map`;
  the published source (send instant redrawn uniformly in each period), 30 seeds, 20 s, the
  seven node counts registered per cell in `results/raw/design_map_predictions.csv`.
  Scored by `analysis/design_map.py --score` → `results/raw/design_map_validation.csv`.
* **Age of information under loss (run after this commit).** `analysis/aoi_under_loss.py` →
  `results/raw/aoi_under_loss.csv`: real lean frames from `LeanSender`, independent frame loss
  p ∈ {0, 0.01, 0.05, 0.10}, `LeanReceiver`; Λ = 50, b = 4 (the design) and b = 1 (the
  baseline, beyond the plan, for comparison); 30 seeds × 240 frames. A frame leaves when its
  last record is taken and is verified T_ch + t_v = 3.7502 ms later (worst case, as Eq. 2).

## Computed before this registration — arithmetic, labelled so

These follow from the frame lengths and Eq. (2) alone; nothing that will be run can move them.

1. **Six cells admit no frame at all**, baseline included: (10, 40), (10, 60), (10, 80),
   (10, 100), (20, 40), (25, 40). Eq. (2) counts the whole filling period, so a record rate of
   10 /s cannot meet a deadline of 100 ms even with one record per frame (100 + 3.75 ms). They
   are reported as **infeasible**, not as "ratio 1.0".
2. **Gain region.** On every feasible cell, b* ≥ 2 exactly when Λ·D_max > 2; every cell with
   Λ·D_max ≤ 2 has b* = 1 and the design's frame IS the baseline's (ratio 1.0 by construction).
   In general b* ≥ 2 needs Λ·D_max ≥ 2 + Λ(T_ch + t_v); no cell of this grid falls between.
3. **MTU.** The frame fits 1500 B with up to **151** records at Λ ≥ 20 (spacing ≤ 50 ms: every
   delta record is 9 B) and **135** at Λ = 10 (100 ms spacing: deltas grow to ~10 B), so the
   plan's "never binds below 151 records" fails at Λ = 10 by arithmetic. It never binds in any
   cell: the largest b* of the grid is 49 (580 B).
4. **The model's ratio** (design/baseline crossing) is non-decreasing in b* at every Λ; at
   (100, 100) b* = 9 and the model gives **6.61×**; at (50, 100) it gives 3.54× (the paper's
   ns-3 figure is 124.55 / 35.26 = 3.53×).
5. **Length-independent loss of the frames themselves.** Delta records are 9.0 B at every
   spacing from 10 to 50 ms; the b = 4 frame is 172.999 B at Λ ≥ 20 and 176.056 B at Λ = 10.

## Predictions — the plan's, verbatim

> - The gain region is exactly ΛDmax > 2; every cell with ΛDmax ≤ 2 shows a ratio of 1.0.
> - The ratio grows with b\*. At (100 records/s, 100 ms), b\* = 9 and the ratio exceeds 5×.
> - Simulated Nmax at the 10 validation cells falls within 5% of the event model.
> - Under loss, the mean extra age is about (b/Λ)·p/(1−p): at b = 4 and p = 0.05 that is about
>   4 ms, so the 16.2 ms margin holds on average. The 99th percentile exceeds Dmax whenever
>   two consecutive frames are lost. Report this honestly.

## Predictions — mine, added before any run `[agent]`

* **A2-1 (crossings).** Each registered role's ns-3 crossing lies in the model's ±5 % band
  given in `design_map_predictions.csv` (this is P3 made numerical). The model has met ±3 %
  on 24 earlier crossings (F5, F6, F7), so a miss above 3 % is reported as a degradation even
  inside 5 %.
* **A2-2 (control).** Runs of cells C and D at a node count and seed already in
  `results/raw/ns3_nmax_direct_runs.csv` reproduce it **bit for bit** (same binary, same
  arguments).
* **A2-3 (the tail of the age — where I disagree with the plan's wording).** One lost frame is
  enough to break the deadline: the next update then lands 2b/Λ after the last, a peak age of
  2·80 + 3.75 = 163.75 ms > 100 ms. With independent loss, the share of updates whose peak age
  exceeds D_max is therefore **p itself**, not p². Prediction: at b = 4 the measured share lies
  in the 95 % binomial interval of p at p = 0.01, 0.05, 0.10, and is 0 at p = 0. Consequences:
  the **95th** percentile exceeds D_max at p = 0.10 and sits at the boundary at p = 0.05; the
  99th exceeds it at p = 0.05 and 0.10 and sits at the boundary at p = 0.01.
* **A2-4 (delay to verification).** The delay of each verified record to its verification does
  not depend on p: its mean differs by less than 0.1 ms between p = 0 and p = 0.10, its maximum
  stays at 60 ms + generator jitter + 3.75 ms. A lost record is never verified; loss shows in V
  and in the age, not in the delay.
* **A2-5 (time-average age).** Mean age = 3.75 + 80(1 + p)/(2(1 − p)) ms within ±1 ms
  (43.75, 44.56, 47.96, 52.64 ms).
* **A2-6 (the baseline, b = 1).** Peak age 20 ms + 3.75 ms at p = 0; exceeding 100 ms needs
  four consecutive losses (share ≤ p⁴ = 10⁻⁴ at p = 0.10, so 0 or 1 of ~7 000 updates).

## Disclosure

* The map and the model crossings were computed before this commit; they ARE the predictions.
  The map was computed twice (the second time with the extra cell); the ten plan cells came out
  identical, as the model is seeded.
* The AoI harness was run once on an unregistered setting to check it: p = 0.3, seed 99, 40
  frames (peaks 75.75–333.75 ms, record delays 3.75–68.75 ms). No registered setting was run.
* No ns-3 run of any `MB*`/`MD*` cell exists. The scenario binary is the published one.
* Literature: two searches for a closed form of peak or average age under independent erasures
  of periodic updates found none stated for this case; the formulas above are the standard
  renewal decomposition of age, E[X²]/2E[X] (Yates et al. 2021, held and cited by the paper),
  applied to a geometric number of frame periods. They are arithmetic, not a result of this
  work.

## What this cannot show

The channel is the published one: one collision domain, equal power (A1 changes that). T_ch is
held at its worst value from one load; at other loads it is smaller, so b* is conservative. The
record walk at 10–13 ms spacing is the generator's, not a flight's (A9 covers real records).
