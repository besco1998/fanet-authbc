# C1 — each sender alone: the radios' own MAC timing, from the sniffer (calibration)

*Registered 2026-11-27, before the session. Decided by Mohamed on 2026-11-23 (`docs/DECISIONS.md`,
"After the gate S0 failed"): C1 runs with the TP-Link, completeness stated per sender, gaps that
straddle a missed frame excluded by sequence number, the sensitivity stated. Code:
`analysis/hw_mac_timing.py` (`tests/test_hw_mac_timing.py`).*

## What kind of session this is

**Calibration: nothing here is scored.**
* Its numbers become inputs of the parameter file (the window and backoff rule, DIFS, the slot,
  the airtime, each board's link loss), which Mohamed reviews before any validation is registered.
* No session that sets a parameter scores a prediction that depends on it.
* The ≥ 99 % rule does not apply to C1 (DECISIONS); C2, C3 and V3 keep it.

## The session (`experiments/channel24/plan_c1.txt`, 33 windows, about 16.5 minutes)

* **The layout:** W1s's 1 m triangle (L2), as recorded at the boards in `geometry.json` (which
  board is where, heights, orientation, the sniffer's place). Every power is 15 dBm, set before the
  first window and read back.
* **Rate checks:** R1–R3 before and after.
* **Per board, in turn:**
  * **Q:** saturated, 600 frames/s offered by the redrawn sender, at 145, 300, 600 and 1 000 B
    payload;
  * **L:** at 50 frames/s, the same four sizes (the link loss per pair and size, from the Pis);
  * **K:** back-to-back, 5 000 frames/s offered, so that the socket always holds a frame, at 145 B.
* Each window is 25 s. The sniffer is the TP-Link (the TSF build), recording from 60 s before to
  60 s after.

## Reported (`python analysis/hw_mac_timing.py SESSION` → `results/raw/c1_<session>.csv`)

**Per window and sender:**
* **completeness:** the sniffer's share of the frames sent;
* **the start-to-start interval** between consecutive sequence numbers, both captured, from TSFT:
  its mean, beside the receivers' cycle, and its 1st percentile;
* **the gap,** the interval less the airtime at 1 Mb/s with the long preamble (192 µs + 8 µs per
  MPDU byte):
  * its mean and median;
  * its floor, the 1st percentile;
  * the lattice σ: the smallest lag within 80 % of the highest autocorrelation peak of the 1 µs
    histogram, between 5 and 40 µs;
  * the share within σ/2 of the floor, sent with no backoff;
* **the backoff index** k = round((gap − floor)/σ):
  * W − 1, where the histogram (k ≥ 1) falls below half its plateau (the median count of
    k = 1 … 8) for three indices running;
  * the share at k = 0;
  * the share beyond 128 slots.
* **The sensitivity to missed frames:** the gap's mean and the share with no backoff, on the
  "strict" pairs only (both neighbours captured too), beside the full set.

**Per board, across sizes (Q windows):** the interval's 1st percentile against the MPDU length.
* **The slope:** 8 µs/B means 1 Mb/s.
* **The intercept:** preamble + DIFS. Expected 242 µs for the long preamble and the standard's
  50 µs, 220 µs with 28 µs, 146 µs with the short preamble. The preamble cannot be read from this
  driver's radiotap.

**The sending pattern's part in the no-backoff share:** K against Q at 145 B, per board.

## My expectations `[agent]`

* **The slope** is 8.0 µs/B within 0.1.
* **The intercept** is near 242 µs (long preamble, DIFS 50).
* **The Pi 4s:**
  * floor 50 µs, lattice 20 µs;
  * a no-backoff share of about 40–50 % at 600 frames/s offered, **falling** with the back-to-back
    sender if frames reaching an idle medium are what skip the backoff (W1s's hypothesis).
  * If it does not fall, the share is the MAC's own behaviour.
* **The Pi 3:** floor above 50 µs and a small no-backoff share.

## Disclosure

* **Seen before this registration, in the gate S0's six saturated rate checks** (`predictions/S0.md`;
  the analysis run on them while it was written):
  * the Pi 4s' floor 50.0 µs, lattice 20 µs, W − 1 13, no-backoff share 0.43–0.50, tail beyond
    128 slots 0.04–1.7 %;
  * the Pi 3's floor 93 µs, lattice 20 µs, W − 1 18–19, no-backoff share 0.038, tail 2.2–3.7 %.
* **Two estimators were revised after seeing those windows:**
  * the lattice, which had picked a harmonic, 40 µs, in one window: now the smallest lag within
    80 % of the peak;
  * W − 1, which the 99th percentile of k read as 93–198 because of the long tail: now the
    plateau rule.
  * Both were tested on synthetic gaps with a known slot and window.
* **Also known:** S0's, D1a's and D1b's completeness (the sniffer misses 1–11 %, drifting).
* **None of C1's own windows has been run.**
