# A9 — richer records, three airframes, 50 Hz flights

*Registered 2026-10-20 before any registered flight. Plan: `docs/AUTHBC Revision Plan.md`, A9.
Outcome appended below a line at the end.*

## What is run

* **Flights.** PX4 v1.17.0 software-in-the-loop with its built-in simulator SIH (no Gazebo):
  `sihsim_quadx`, `sihsim_airplane` (fixed wing), `sihsim_standard_vtol`; **missions 1–10 of
  each**, `analysis/px4_airframe_flight.py`. A mission is a closed polygon of 4–6 legs whose
  size, heading and commanded speed are drawn from a generator seeded with (airframe, number):
  multicopter 5–15 m/s and 150–400 m legs, fixed wing and VTOL 15–25 m/s (VTOL 15–22) and
  600–1 500 m legs; uploaded over MAVLink and flown in AUTO.MISSION, ended at the last waypoint.
  Logged at 20 ms: position, attitude, body rates, GNSS, airspeed, wind, mission progress,
  battery, status, thrust. Fixed-wing settings needed in SIH: no landing pattern required
  (`MIS_TKO_LAND_REQ 0`), no airspeed sensor (`SYS_HAS_NUM_ASPD 0`).
* **Records.** `analysis/record_size_sensitivity.py`: in-flight records on the 20 ms grid with 9
  (the paper's), 15 (+ attitude, body rates) and 25 fields (+ GNSS fix type, satellites,
  accuracies, airspeed, wind, mission item, a status word, thrust), coded as the lean format
  codes its own (keyframe varints, zig-zag deltas), frames of b = 1–4 at their exact lengths,
  timestamps placed an hour into a flight (the sizing convention's origin). On nine fields the
  code reproduces the encoder: the same record-stream bytes and the same frame lengths as
  `wire_v2` encodes (`tests/test_record_size_sensitivity.py`).
  **Stated difference from the plan:** its 15-field set lists GNSS fix quality too, which makes
  16; fix quality is moved to the 25-field set.
* **Capacity** by the no-fit closed form (docs/02 §6g; within 5.5 % of the eighteen simulated
  crossings), baseline at 50 frames/s, design at 12.5. The plan names Eq. (6); the closed form
  is used because Eq. (6) left its band for frames outside its fitted range in A15.
* The plan's "public Flight Review logs faster than 5 Hz": none is added — the pinned selection
  rule of the twelve logs already in the paper found none, and widening it now would be a rule
  chosen after looking.

## Predictions — the plan's, verbatim

> - At 25 fields (keyframe about 60 B, delta about 25 B), the byte reduction is still about 65%.
> - The capacity ratio stays near 3×, because frame count, not bytes, dominates.
> - Fixed-wing deltas reach 10–13 B at 50 Hz, about 1–2 B more than the quadrotor.

Scored as: (1) 25 fields, mean over the 30 flights: keyframe 50–70 B, delta 20–30 B, saving
60–70 %; (2) ratio at 25 fields within 2.6–3.4; (3) fixed-wing 9-field delta mean 10–13 B and
1–2 B above the quadrotor's.

## Mine `[agent]`

* **A9-1.** At 9 fields the quadrotor's delta is 9.0–10 B (the paper's 9.0 from one SIH flight
  and the generator), and every airframe's 9-field saving lies within 2 points of the paper's
  70.3 %.
* **A9-2 (the ratio falls slowly with the record).** Ratios by the closed form: 9 fields ≈ 3.6,
  15 ≈ 3.3, 25 ≈ 3.0. Batching keeps a 2× capacity advantage until the delta reaches roughly
  100 B a record: the frame count, not the record, sets the gain.
* **A9-3.** Fixed-wing and VTOL deltas exceed the quadrotor's at every field count (higher speed
  and attitude change per 20 ms).
