# A6 — the frame protocol in Tamarin

*Registered 2026-10-20 before any proof attempt. The model was only parsed and checked for
well-formedness (`tamarin-prover formal/authbc.spthy`, without `--prove`). Plan:
`docs/AUTHBC Revision Plan.md`, A6. Outcomes appended below a line at the end.*

## Model (`formal/authbc.spthy`)

Tamarin 1.12.0 with Maude 3.5.1 (release assets checked against GitHub's sha256 digests;
installed under `~/.local/opt`, not in the repository). Dolev–Yao network; a Reveal rule hands
any sender's signing key to the adversary. A sender signs ⟨'authbc', S, seq, link, data, t⟩:
a strictly increasing natural-number sequence, the hash of its previous frame, the records
(one fresh term for all b of them), and the stamp of a global clock that only advances. A
receiver verifies, accepts only a newer sequence number, keeps what it accepted, records a gap
when a number is skipped, and keeps two valid frames with one number as evidence. With
`-D=TIME` it also refuses a frame stamped more than one tick ago (the A5 rule, abstracted).

**Assumption A1, stated and not proven:** canonical encoding is injective (Tamarin's tuples are
free constructors). Supported in the code by the byte-identity tests of the canonical encoder
and the exhaustive bit-flip census of one frame.

## Predictions — the plan's, verbatim

> lemmas 1–4 are proven; lemma 5 shows the attack first and then its fix. This turns two
> stated limitations into one proof plus one demonstrated mitigation.

(Lemmas, as the plan numbers them: 1 origin and integrity — injective agreement on the frame
content unless the sender's key is revealed; 2 no frame accepted twice; 3 equivocation
evidence implies the sender's key was revealed; 4 continuity — a gap is detected; 5 the
delayed-replay trace without the time rule, gone with it.)

Scored per lemma, in each variant, as Tamarin's verdict with `--prove`.

Expectation on effort, written down so that a failure of automation is not confused with a
failure of the property: counters and hash chains can keep Tamarin's backward search from
terminating; the plan foresaw helper lemmas. Each variant gets a budget of one hour of
automatic proof (`--prove`, default heuristic). A lemma not settled in the budget is reported
as **not settled**, never as proven, and helper lemmas added afterwards are reported as added
afterwards.

**What is by construction and what is not.** `a_skipped_frame_is_detected` follows almost
directly from the receiver's rules (a skipped number takes the gap rule); its proof says the
rules are complete, not that an adversary is defeated. `continuity_follows_the_senders_chain`,
`origin_and_integrity`, `equivocation_implies_revealed_key` and `bounded_delay_freshness` rest
on the signature and the hash and are the security content.

## What this cannot show

A symbolic model: perfect cryptography, no timing side channels, no implementation bugs; one
abstract clock (the A5 harness measures the real window with clock error). The model has one
signature scheme; Ed25519 and ECDSA P-256 are the same here.
