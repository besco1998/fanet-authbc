# P1 — the overload probe: does the TP-Link sniffer reach 99 % when it is farther from the senders?

*Registered 2026-11-23, before the session, after the gate S0 failed on capture completeness
(`predictions/S0.md`, Outcome). Decided by Mohamed on 2026-11-23 (`docs/DECISIONS.md`, "After the
gate S0 failed"). Code: `analysis/s0_diag.py` (`tests/test_s0_diag.py`). Kit: as S0
(`run_sniffer2.sh`, the TSF build, `run_window_plan2.sh` with `chip_check.sh`).*

## The hypothesis

The sniffer misses 1–11 % of the frames the Pis receive. One explanation is that the frames, at
−39 to −49 dBm at 15 to 85 cm, overload the adapter's receiver.

## The session (`experiments/channel24/plan_p1.txt`, 4 windows, run once per placement)

* **The Pis fixed** in W1s's 1 m triangle, as in D1b: pi-A and pi-B swapped, the Pi 3 unmoved.
* **The sniffer (Blue 2 with the TP-Link) moved** to about **3 m**, then to about **5 m**, from the
  triangle's centre (or behind a wall).
  * The distances, heights and orientation are recorded at the boards in each placement's
    `geometry.json`.
* **Windows:**
  * each Pi alone at 50 frames/s, 145 B, 23 s (1 000 frames), all at 15 dBm;
  * then the saturated pi-A + pi-B pair, 25 s.

## Registered

* **Reported per placement and sender:**
  * the sniffer's share of the sender's 1 000 frames, beside each Pi's;
  * the sniffer's mean signal;
  * the number of frames flagged with a bad checksum (the driver passes them or not).
* **The rule:** a placement at which **every sender's share is ≥ 0.99** is re-gated there (S0b,
  registered before it runs). Otherwise the TP-Link stays a timing-only sniffer under the per-sender
  rule (DECISIONS), and the ≥ 99 % sessions wait for the AR9271 until 2026-11-30.

## My expectations `[agent]`, and what the data in hand already show

* **Overload is not favoured by the data already seen** (disclosed):
  * in S0, pi-A at 6 dBm (−47.7 dBm at the sniffer) was missed 5.4 %, against 4.8 % at 15 dBm;
  * in D1b, pi-A at −49 dBm was missed 5.2 %, against 2.9 % at −40 dBm in D1a.
* **So I expect no placement to reach 0.99,** and the misses to stay at a few percent, drifting
  between windows.
* **Bad-checksum frames:** 0, since the driver as built drops them.

## Disclosure

* S0, D1a and D1b, with their outcomes and diagnostics, are known.

## Outcome — added 2026-11-27, after the session; nothing above this line was changed

* **Only the extra placement ran.** Mohamed chose on 2026-11-23 to run the place where he had set
  the sniffer as an extra, outside the rule: about 50 cm from the Pi 3 and pi-A, about 110 cm from
  pi-B.
  * On 2026-11-27 he was not at the boards ("do your best without me touching the boards";
    DECISIONS, "Runs without Mohamed at the boards").
  * So **the registered placements, about 3 m and about 5 m, were not run:** they need the sniffer
    moved. **The rule could not be applied.**
* **The session:** `results/hw/frame_campaign/p1x_2026-11-27`, 02:51:12–02:53:06.
  * The boards had all rebooted together on 2026-11-25 22:27 and had not been moved.
  * A first preflight said RETRY (every cycle 1 666 µs, F129's first session after a boot); the
    second said GO (2 025.5 / 2 033.1 / 2 340.6 µs).
  * Frame burst read 0 throughout; no chip stop; tcpdump dropped nothing; the sniffer stayed on one
    boot.

| sender | the sniffer's share of 1 000 frames | the Pis' shares | the sniffer's mean signal |
|---|---|---|---|
| pi-A | **0.962** | 0.999, 1.000 | −48.0 dBm |

* **No sender reached 0.99.** The extra placement would not have qualified for a re-gate either.
* **Bad-checksum frames: 0** in the whole capture, the saturated pair included. The driver as built
  passes none.
* **The independent recompute:** a separate reader, sharing no project code (radiotap skipped by
  its length field, the 802.11, IP and UDP headers parsed by hand, the spans from the Pis' files),
  gave the same three shares to four places.

### My expectations, against the outcome

* **"No placement reaches 0.99":** right for the one that ran.
* **"The misses stay at a few percent":** right, 2.6–5.5 %.

### Seen after scoring — not a result

* **The strongest signal was again the best captured:** pi-B at −35.2 dBm, 0.974. That runs
  against overload once more, but one placement cannot test a trend.
* **The signals do not match the stated distances.** pi-B, given as the farthest (about 110 cm), is
  the loudest by 9–13 dB.
  * Either the distances were given by the boards' names before the D1b swap, or the radio geometry
    differs from the tape (as in W1s).
  * It is recorded, not resolved.
