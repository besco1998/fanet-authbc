# P1 — the overload probe: does the TP-Link sniffer reach 99 % when it is farther from the senders?

*Registered 2026-11-23, before the session, after the gate S0 failed on capture completeness
(`predictions/S0.md`, Outcome). Decided by Mohamed on 2026-11-23 (`docs/DECISIONS.md`, "After the
gate S0 failed"). Code: `analysis/s0_diag.py` (`tests/test_s0_diag.py`). Kit: as S0
(`run_sniffer2.sh`, the TSF build, `run_window_plan2.sh` with `chip_check.sh`).*

## The hypothesis

The sniffer misses 1–11 % of the frames the Pis receive. One explanation is that the frames, at
−39 to −49 dBm at 15 to 85 cm, overload the adapter's receiver.

## The session (`experiments/channel24/plan_p1.txt`, 4 windows, run once per placement)

* **The Pis fixed** in W1s's 1 m triangle, as in D1b: pi-A and pi-B swapped, the Pi 3 unmoved.
* **The sniffer (Blue 2 with the TP-Link) moved** to about **3 m**, then to about **5 m**, from the
  triangle's centre (or behind a wall).
  * The distances, heights and orientation are recorded at the boards in each placement's
    `geometry.json`.
* **Windows:**
  * each Pi alone at 50 frames/s, 145 B, 23 s (1 000 frames), all at 15 dBm;
  * then the saturated pi-A + pi-B pair, 25 s.

## Registered

* **Reported per placement and sender:**
  * the sniffer's share of the sender's 1 000 frames, beside each Pi's;
  * the sniffer's mean signal;
  * the number of frames flagged with a bad checksum (the driver passes them or not).
* **The rule:** a placement at which **every sender's share is ≥ 0.99** is re-gated there (S0b,
  registered before it runs). Otherwise the TP-Link stays a timing-only sniffer under the per-sender
  rule (DECISIONS), and the ≥ 99 % sessions wait for the AR9271 until 2026-11-30.

## My expectations `[agent]`, and what the data in hand already show

* **Overload is not favoured by the data already seen** (disclosed):
  * in S0, pi-A at 6 dBm (−47.7 dBm at the sniffer) was missed 5.4 %, against 4.8 % at 15 dBm;
  * in D1b, pi-A at −49 dBm was missed 5.2 %, against 2.9 % at −40 dBm in D1a.
* **So I expect no placement to reach 0.99,** and the misses to stay at a few percent, drifting
  between windows.
* **Bad-checksum frames:** 0, since the driver as built drops them.
