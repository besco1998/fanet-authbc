# Direction C survey — protocol, committed BEFORE the data

*Written 2026-08-07, **before** the corpus is assembled or any paper beyond the nine pilot entries
is swept. Committed as its own commit so the ordering is checkable in git history.*

> ⚠️ **Why this file exists at all.** Finding F40 forced the withdrawal of a pre-registration claim
> on the earlier phase-artifact work: the header cited
> `scratchpad/C1_EXPECTATIONS.md`, that file had never been committed, and reconstructing it after
> the results were known would have *manufactured* evidence. The lesson is not "be more careful" —
> it is **commit the protocol before the data exists**, exactly as the E5 success criterion was
> (`3354ec1`, two days before `a51486a`). This is that commit for the survey.

---

## 1. The claim under test

> **H1.** Published ns-3 LoRa/LoRaWAN simulation studies do not report the replication information
> a reader needs in order to judge a threshold crossing — seed count, number of runs, confidence
> intervals, or dispersion.

⚠️ **H1 is a claim about *reporting*, not about practice.** It must never be phrased as "these
authors did not replicate". They may have run many seeds and not said so; the deficiency being
measured is that a reader cannot tell. Any wording that implies otherwise is a misstatement of the
result and is out of scope for this survey.

### What would falsify it

If a substantial fraction of the corpus (**pre-specified: ≥ 25 %**) states a seed count, run count,
confidence interval or dispersion measure, H1 is not supported and will be reported as such. The
pilot ran 9/9 in favour; a null or mixed result on the full corpus is a publishable outcome and
will be reported without reframing.

---

## 2. Inclusion criteria

A paper enters the corpus if **all** hold:

1. it presents **simulation** results for LoRa or LoRaWAN;
2. the simulator is **ns-3** (stated in the paper);
3. the full text is **obtainable** and **machine-readable** (see §4 on extraction failure);
4. it is not a duplicate of an entry already in the corpus.

Papers are sought without regard to whether they appear to report replication. ⚠️ **Search queries
must not contain replication terms** (`seed`, `confidence interval`, `Monte Carlo`, …), because
querying on the dependent variable would bias the corpus. Queries name the *topic* only.

## 3. The measurement

A fixed keyword set, specified **here, in advance**, applied to the full extracted text:

```
seed | seeds | repetition | repeat(ed|s) | replicat* | Monte Carlo | averaged over |
confidence interval | standard deviation | error bar | independent runs | simulation runs |
N runs / N trials / N experiments (numeric)
```

Each hit is **read in context by hand** before it counts. This is required, not optional: the pilot
already produced a false positive — *"Hybrid Automatic Repeat reQuest"* matching `repeat` — and
Van den Abeele et al. matched `average` five times, every occurrence referring to the **traffic
model** (Poisson rate, spreading-factor distribution) rather than to averaging across runs.

### Verdicts

| verdict | meaning |
|---|---|
| `REPORTS` | states a seed/run count, CI, or dispersion measure, confirmed in context |
| `NONE` | keyword sweep returns no hit, **or** every hit is a confirmed false positive |
| `UNREADABLE` | text extraction yields < 2000 characters — **excluded from the denominator** |

⚠️ **`UNREADABLE` is not evidence of anything.** A scanned, image-only PDF returns zero keyword hits
for a reason that has nothing to do with the authors' reporting. The pilot already hit this: Zirak
et al. extracts **5 characters**. Counting such a paper as `NONE` would manufacture support for H1,
which is the single most likely way this survey could produce a false result. The harness therefore
refuses to emit `NONE` when extraction is short, and the count is reported as
`NONE / (REPORTS + NONE)` with `UNREADABLE` listed separately.

## 4. Sample size and stopping rule

Target **56** papers, the figure already quoted in `docs/NEXT_STEPS.md`. The survey stops at 56, or
when the accessible open-access pool is exhausted — whichever comes first. ⚠️ **If it stops short,
the achieved n is reported as the achieved n.** Stopping early because the pattern looks clear, or
continuing until it looks clearer, are both forbidden.

## 5. Artifacts

`results/raw/direction_c_survey.csv` — one row per paper: identifier, ns-3 (y/n), extracted
character count, hit count, verdict, and the context string for every hit that was adjudicated.
Generated by `analysis/direction_c_survey.py` so the sweep is reproducible; the manual adjudication
is recorded in the CSV rather than left in someone's head.

## 6. Declared in advance, so it cannot be added later

* The **jitter value** (currently 1 s) is a *choice*, not a derivation, and this survey does not
  make it defensible. That remains open and must not be presented as settled by this work.
* This survey measures **reporting practice**. It does not establish that the frozen-phase artifact
  changes any *published* conclusion — only that a reader could not detect it from what is printed.
* Nine papers are already swept (the pilot). They are retained in the corpus and **flagged as
  pilot**, since they were selected before this protocol was written.

# SWEEP LOG (appended 2026-08-08; nothing above this line was changed)

*The pre-registered protocol is unchanged. This section records what the sweep actually did, so the
sampling is auditable rather than asserted.*

## Achieved n = 14 (target was 56)

⚠️ **Reported as the achieved n, per §4.** The sweep stopped because the accessible open-access pool
was exhausted, not because the pattern looked clear.

**How papers were sought.** arXiv API, topic-only queries, per §2 — no replication term ever
appeared in a query, since querying on the dependent variable would bias the corpus. Queries used:
`LoRaWAN AND ns-3`, `LoRa AND ns-3`, `LoRa AND "network simulator 3"`, `LoRaWAN AND simulation`,
`LoRa AND "discrete event simulation"`, `LoRaWAN AND "spreading factor" AND simulator`,
`LoRa AND collision AND simulation AND gateway`, `LoRaWAN AND "capture effect"`,
`LoRa AND multi-hop AND simulation`.

## Sought and NOT included — every exclusion with its reason

| candidate | reason |
|---|---|
| arXiv 2509.02811 | **duplicate** of `traspadini2025_lora_ntn_ns3` (criterion 4) |
| arXiv 2507.23342 (FAST-LoRa) | **fails criterion 2** — ns-3 appears only as related work and as a performance foil; the simulator is their own framework |
| arXiv 2008.09019 (Long-Lived LoRa) | **fails criterion 2** — no ns-3 use (0 mentions) |
| arXiv 1811.05386, 1811.06345 | **fail criterion 3** — no PDF retrievable from arXiv or its export mirror on repeated attempts |
| MDPI / PMC / IEEE candidates | **fail criterion 3 in this environment** — the hosts refuse scripted retrieval, returning challenge pages. ⚠️ They were **not** fetched via a summarising tool: a summary is not the text, and sweeping one would risk manufacturing `NONE` verdicts, which §3 names as the single most likely way this survey produces a false result |

⚠️ **This is a real sampling limitation and it biases toward arXiv-hosted work.** Whether arXiv
preprints report replication better or worse than paywalled venues is not something this corpus can
answer, and no claim is made either way.

## Result

**12 NONE / 2 REPORTS out of 14 counted; REPORTS = 14.3 %**, below the pre-registered 25 %
falsification threshold, so H1 is supported at the achieved n.

Both `REPORTS` verdicts are counter-examples to our own hypothesis and both are direct quotations:

* `klimiashvili2020` — *"the average of 50 independent runs over channel realization and nodes' position"*
* `reliable_uplink_lorawan_dcoss2019` — *"the experimental results ... are collected from 100 simulation runs, and the average is calculated"*

## ⚠️ Hand adjudication earned its place

The keyword sweep alone would have been wrong on three papers:

* `ramanathan2025` matched `seed` on **"Seed Studio WM1302"** — a hardware vendor's name. A purely
  mechanical sweep would have scored a REPORTS on a company.
* `lauridsen2019` produced five hits, four of them the LoRaWAN protocol's `repetitions` parameter or
  ns-3 *execution time*. The closest call in the corpus, adjudicated `NONE` because it states no
  count, CI or dispersion — while publishing its implementation so readers can replicate, which is
  better practice than most of the corpus and simply is not what H1 measures.
* `batteryless_2021` matched on the protocol's message-repetition feature.
